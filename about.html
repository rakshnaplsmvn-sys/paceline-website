Our Story

We opened Paceline in 2015 with one goal — to build the running shop we'd always wanted. A decade in, we're still hand-picking every shoe, every layer and every accessory for runners just like us.

01 · WHY WE EXIST

We're all
about the run.

It wasn't until 2015 that we realised there was a gap. We were here for the preparation, the improvement and the gear — but not a place that brought all those things together. Our tried-and-tested range covers road, trail, track and field, apparel and accessories, with no compromise on performance or style.

Paceline is small and independent, but the community around it is anything but. We're on our own running club, meet local athletes, and partner with local charities to do our bit for runners in Glasgow and beyond.

Whatever mile you're on, we want to be your shop. Come say hello.

— Rae & Jamie, Founders

02 · WHAT WE STAND FOR

Three things we won't compromise on.

01

Curated, not sold to us.

Every brand and every shoe earns its place. We test and choose kit for real runners, from first 5Ks to weekend adventures.

02

Community over transaction.

From run clubs to local events, we're here to build a running community that supports every pace and every goal.

03

Craft in every detail.

From how we help you choose to how we pack each order, we believe the details should make your running life better.

03 · THE SEASONS IN

How we got here.

2015

Doors open.

Paceline opens its doors in central Glasgow with a small, hand-picked selection of running essentials.

2018

The clinic launches.

We start helping runners find the right kit and understand what works for their own running style.

2022

Weekly run club.

Every Wednesday, runners of all paces meet for a friendly community run through Glasgow.

2026

40 brands & counting.

Ten years in, we're still hand-picking every product, layer, shoe and accessory.
